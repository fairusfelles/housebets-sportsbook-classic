Your big idea:
A second, deliberately different direction: the classic professional bookmaker. Racing green, ivory and brass, serif display type and dense tabular odds — the look of a bookmaker that has taken bets for a hundred years, rebuilt inside the Housebets shell. Where entry one is a modern app, this one is a trading desk: calm, credible, numbers first. All five pages, singles-only USDT slip, odds-change accept flow, suspended markets, bet placed and failed states, and cash out.
